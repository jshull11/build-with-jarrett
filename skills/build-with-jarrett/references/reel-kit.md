# Reel kit

Every stage is a post. Build with Jarrett is designed so people can film their build as
they go: each page is made to look good on a phone screen recording, and each ends with
a 9:16 reel card.

## The reel card

The last section of every page. A 9:16 frame (built into the shell as `.reel`) that
looks finished as a screenshot or as the final shot of a recording:

- **One big headline** for the stage, at most eight words ("My app has a name").
- **The hero visual** of the stage (the one sentence, the icon, the magic-moment
  screen, three storyboard frames, the milestone track).
- **The stage label**: "Step 3 of 10 · Brand".
- **The brand mark** at the bottom: "Built with Jarrett · @buildwithjarrett".
- The app's own colours and fonts (neutral defaults before stage 3).

Rules: large type, high contrast, nothing important within the top 12% or bottom 20%
(Instagram's interface covers those areas), no small print.

## Filming tips to give the user

- Record the screen (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording; Mac: `Cmd+Shift+5`).
- Scroll slowly and steadily; pause two seconds on the hero of each page.
- Phone recordings of the page look best: open the page link on the phone.
- End on the reel card for two seconds.
- Add a voiceover or on-screen text with the hook.
- Hooks work best in the first two seconds.

## What to give at each stage

At every gate, give a **reel moment** block:

```
REEL · Step N of 10
Hook: "<first line, under 10 words>"
Shots: 1) … 2) … 3) …
Caption: <1–2 lines> · Built with @buildwithjarrett #buildwithjarrett
```

Suggestions by stage (adapt to their app and their own words):

| Stage | Hook idea | Shots |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Interview | "I told AI my app idea. Here's what happened." | Talking about the idea → Claude's playback → "Let's build it" |
| 2 Plan | "AI turned my rambling into a real app plan." | Scroll the Plan page → the 60-second timeline → the reel card |
| 3 Brand | "AI helped me name my app." | The name finalists → the palette → the icon reveal |
| 4 Design | "This is what my app looks like." | The design system → each screen → the magic moment |
| 5 Storyboard | "Every second of my app's first minute." | Slow sideways scroll through the frames → the magic moment |
| 6 Build plan | "How I'm building an app with zero coding experience." | The milestone track → the system diagram → "Starting now" |
| 7 Build | "My app is on my phone." | Scanning the QR code → the app opening → the magic moment live |
| 8 Ship | "My app is on TestFlight." | The build finishing → TestFlight install → opening it |
| 9 Launch | "I built an app with AI. It's live." | The App Store listing → the magic moment → the Journey page |

The **Journey page** is the series' anchor: one page with all ten stages that grows
every step. It makes a good recap post and a good "link in bio".

## Honesty

Reels show the real app and the real process. No made-up download numbers, reviews or
results.

# Stage 1: Interview

A conversation in Claude Code, not a form. The goal is enough to write a strong plan,
and no more.

## How to run it

1. **Open wide.** Ask them to talk about the idea for a couple of minutes, any way they
   like: who it's for, why they want it, apps they love. Suggest voice dictation.
2. **Fill the gaps.** From what they said, work out which of the questions below are
   still unanswered. Ask one or two at a time, in plain words. Offer a suggested answer
   they can accept ("Sounds like the magic moment is X. Right?").
3. **Push back gently** where an answer would hurt the app (see below). Recommend, then
   let them decide.
4. **Play it back** in five lines: the one sentence, who it's for, the magic moment, the
   share moment, how it makes money. Ask "Did I get it?" Then go to stage 2.

Aim for 10–15 minutes. Don't ask anything you can decide yourself (technology, file
structure, libraries).

## What you need by the end

1. **Who it's for.** A specific person, not "everyone".
2. **The feeling.** Which core desire it serves: being liked, knowing what people think
   of you, looking cool, feeling better, getting ahead, knowing something others don't.
   Bonus: a pain it removes. Pain sells better than vitamins.
3. **The one sentence.** What it does, for whom, ideally under ten words. It'll go on
   the first screen, the first App Store screenshot and every ad.
4. **The magic moment.** The "wow, this works" moment. Reachable within about 60
   seconds of opening the app.
5. **The share moment.** What would a user show a friend? What does the friend do next?
6. **Day two.** Why do they come back tomorrow? A reminder, a streak, something new to
   see, something they can't predict?
7. **v1 scope.** The 3–6 screens that matter. Everything else goes on the cut list.
8. **Accounts and data.** Does it need logins, saved data across devices, or AI? (You
   decide the technology; you only need to know what it must do.)
9. **Money.** Free, a soft paywall (free tier plus paid) or a hard paywall (pay after
   the magic moment)? Weekly, monthly or annual? A rough answer is fine.
10. **The look.** Three adjectives, one thing it should NOT look like, and two or three
    apps whose feel they love.
11. **Working name**, if they have one. Naming properly happens in stage 3.

## When to push back

- **No clear one sentence.** Offer two or three versions and let them pick.
- **Magic moment takes minutes.** Suggest a faster first taste.
- **Too many features.** Pick the core with them; move the rest to the cut list. Say
  why: every feature makes the app harder to explain and slower to ship.
- **"It's for everyone."** Ask who'd download it first, this week.
- **A sharing plan that needs people to post private things.** Find the part that's
  safe to show (a result, a goal, a streak).
- **Anything that fakes activity or users.** It's a hard no; offer an honest version.
- **Crowded category.** That's fine, but the plan must say how the first three minutes
  will beat everyone else's.

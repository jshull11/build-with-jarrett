# Stage 5: Storyboard

The design shows what screens look like. The storyboard shows **what happens**, frame by
frame: the onboarding from the first open to the magic moment and beyond, and the main
screens a returning user moves through. It's the script the build follows.

## Two sequences

### A. Onboarding: first open to the share moment

Usually 6–10 frames. The standard order (adapt it):

1. Splash: the one sentence and one button.
2. One or two setup questions (only what the magic moment needs).
3. A permission, behind a screen that explains why it's useful right now
   (for notifications: "What time do you want a reminder?" then the system prompt).
4. "Now or later?" if the next step needs quiet, time or headphones.
5. The step that produces the magic moment (the wait, made part of the story).
6. **The magic moment.**
7. Paywall, if any.
8. A second, shareable moment (a card with their name, a result, a member number).
9. Any habit-forming permission, now that they've felt why.

### B. Main screens: a returning user

3–6 frames: what someone sees when they open the app on day 2, the core action, the
reward, and what brings them back on day 3.

## Each frame shows

- A phone-sized sketch of the screen in the brand (simpler than the Design: layout and
  key words, so the flow is the star).
- **The clock**: seconds since first open (onboarding only). The magic moment frame
  must land at 60s or less; flag it in red if it doesn't and suggest a cut.
- **What the user does** (one line: "Taps Start").
- **What they feel** (one word: curious, surprised, proud).
- **What the app does** behind the scenes, if anything (asks permission, calls the AI,
  saves).
- Motion, haptic or sound cues for the important frames ("confetti + success haptic").

## The Storyboard page

From the shell, in the brand:

1. Title and the one sentence.
2. Sequence A as a horizontal strip of frames that scrolls sideways on its own (the page
   itself must not scroll sideways), with the clock running along the top. The magic
   moment frame is visibly the peak.
3. Sequence B the same way.
4. A "feelings line" under sequence A: the one-word feelings joined, so the emotional
   arc reads in one glance.
5. Open questions from the flow, if any.
6. Reel card: three key frames side by side, "first open → magic moment".
7. Footer with the brand mark.

A sideways strip is great to film: a slow horizontal scroll through the frames.

## Gate

Approve, or change something? Check together: is the magic moment within 60 seconds,
and is every frame one obvious action?

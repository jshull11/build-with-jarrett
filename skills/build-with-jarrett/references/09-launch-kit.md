# Stage 9: Launch kit

An app shipped into silence doesn't get users. The **Launch kit page** gives the user
everything to launch: the App Store listing, screenshot designs, launch reels and a plan
for the first 1,000 users. Read [growth-and-money](growth-and-money.md) first.

## App Store listing

- **Name** (up to 30 characters) and **subtitle** (up to 30): the subtitle is usually
  the one sentence, shortened.
- **Keywords** (100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces after commas): words people
  would search, not already in the name or subtitle.
- **Description:** the first three lines matter most (that's what shows before "more").
  Lead with the one sentence and the magic moment.
- **Promotional text** (170 characters), changeable without a new build.
- **Category** and age rating notes.
- **Privacy:** what data is collected, matched to what the app actually does. The user
  needs a privacy policy URL; offer to draft one they publish themselves.

## Screenshots

Design **five screenshots** at 1290×2796 (6.9-inch iPhone):

1. The one sentence, big, over the splash or magic-moment screen.
2. The magic moment.
3. The core action.
4. The share moment or a result.
5. A reason to come back (streak, reminder, new content).

Keep them simple: one short line on each, the phone showing the real screen. Draw them
on the page at true proportions so the user can screenshot or export them. Real
in-app screenshots (from the simulator: `Cmd+S` in Simulator) look best inside the frame.

## Launch reels

Three short scripts (15–30 seconds), each with a hook (first 2 seconds), three shots and
a caption:

1. **The pain:** "If you [problem], watch this." Then the app solving it.
2. **The magic moment**, screen-recorded with sound.
3. **The build story:** "I built this app with AI in [N] days." Using the pages from
   every stage. Tag @buildwithjarrett.

## The first 1,000 users

A one-week plan:

- **Day 1:** post the magic-moment reel; aim for one video that hits. Ideally the first
  users know each other.
- **Daily:** one post on each of TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Faceless slideshows work.
  Lead with the pain.
- **Reddit and communities** where the "who it's for" person already hangs out (helpful
  posts, not spam).
- **Personal network:** a direct message to 20 people who fit "who it's for".
- **Track** downloads, magic-moment rate and share rate daily (the analytics from
  stage 6).
- **Make it a moment:** a launch date, a founding-member offer or a challenge gives
  people a reason to join now.

## The Launch kit page

From the shell, in the brand:

1. The listing copy, ready to paste, with character counts.
2. The five screenshots at true proportions.
3. The three reel scripts as cards.
4. The first-week plan as a day-by-day track.
5. Reel card: "Launch day", the app icon and name.
6. Footer with the brand mark.

## Gate and finish

Ask if they want changes. Then update the Journey page to show all ten stages done,
celebrate, and close with:

> You built an app. Post it and tag **@buildwithjarrett**. Questions or stuck? DM
> **@buildwithjarrett** on Instagram.

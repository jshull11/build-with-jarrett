# Build with Jarrett App Builder

A Claude Code plugin that takes you from an app idea to a real, high-quality iPhone app,
one stage at a time. After every stage you get a polished page to look at, approve and
share: your plan, your brand, your design, your storyboard, your build plan and your
launch kit.

**Questions or stuck? DM [@buildwithjarrett](https://instagram.com/buildwithjarrett) on Instagram.**

## Install

In Claude Code, run:

```
/plugin marketplace add jshull11/build-with-jarrett
/plugin install build-with-jarrett@build-with-jarrett
```

Restart Claude Code.

## Update

I keep improving the skill. To get the latest version:

```
/plugin marketplace update build-with-jarrett
/plugin update build-with-jarrett@build-with-jarrett
```

Restart Claude Code after updating.

## Start

Make an empty folder for your app, open Claude Code in it and say:

> Let's build an app with Jarrett. My idea is…

Or type `/build-with-jarrett`. Talking works better than typing: use voice dictation
and ramble about your idea. Close Claude Code any time; next session it picks up where
you left off.

## The ten stages

0. **Welcome**
1. **Interview:** talk through your idea with Claude.
2. **Plan:** your app on one page: the one sentence, the magic moment, the screens.
3. **Brand:** name, icon, colours and fonts.
4. **Design:** your design system and key screens.
5. **Storyboard:** your onboarding and main screens, frame by frame.
6. **Build plan:** the milestones, the tech, what you'll need.
7. **Build:** the app, running on your phone.
8. **Ship:** on TestFlight.
9. **Launch kit:** App Store listing, screenshots, launch reels, first-1,000-users plan.

You approve each stage before the next one starts. Every page ends with a 9:16 card
made for Reels: post your build as you go and tag @buildwithjarrett.

## What you need

- Claude Code (it needs a paid Claude plan).
- A Mac with Node.js 20+. Xcode from the App Store is optional (an iPhone simulator).
- The free **Expo Go** app on your phone.
- Later, if your app needs it: a free Supabase account, and an Apple Developer account
  ($99/year) for TestFlight and the App Store.
- The pages show up as shareable artifacts in the Claude desktop app, or as web pages
  saved in your project's `build-pages/` folder elsewhere.
